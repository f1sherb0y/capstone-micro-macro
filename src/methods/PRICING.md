# Declared supply costs and markup

For each explicitly scheduled batch, cost = quantity × sum(unit costs) +
sum(batch-fixed costs). All unit costs apply to that batch's declared quantity
unit; all amounts use the same currency. Price = cost × (1 + cost markup).
Round upward to the profile's explicitly declared minor currency unit. This is
a cost markup, not a margin on selling price. Zero costs may be supplied with
evidence; output binding prices must still be positive.

Arithmetic uses exact rational representations of finite decimal inputs. The
ceiling calculation uses integer arithmetic and is independent of Decimal's
ambient precision. There is no implicit tax, exchange rate or unit conversion.
Each included service must have an explicit cost component, including a sourced
zero component when included at no additional charge. Fixed costs are charged
per stated batch, not silently per inquiry. Separate business checks establish
the completeness, applicability and validity of those facts.

This mathematical method does not validate real production, quality, transport,
inventory, market conditions or tax compliance. It creates neither a contract
nor an order, reservation, ledger entry or macro receipt.
