# Declared service selection

Consume only offers already checked against the exact service scope, parties,
authority, current qualifications, deliverables, responsibilities and management
capacity. Compare each exact same-currency fee against the declared procurement
cap. Exclude fees greater than the cap; a fee equal to the cap is permitted.
Among the remaining offers select the smallest explicitly supplied ordinal
priority. Preserve all tied best-priority references for clarification. Neither
source order nor a cheaper fee breaks a priority tie. Zero fees are valid when
explicitly offered; unknown fees are never filled with zero.

Use exact decimal fractions, independent of the caller's Decimal precision.
No exchange-rate conversion, fee estimation, inferred preference or cash/debt
calculation is performed. The procurement cap is a permission limit, not
available money. A local choice never constitutes independent acceptance,
appointment, payment, resource commitment or a formal receipt. Real source
production and qualification/method calibration remain separate requirements.
