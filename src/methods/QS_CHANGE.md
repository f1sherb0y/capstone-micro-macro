# QS sourced change valuation v1

Exact signed quantity difference × nonnegative original unit rate for disjoint declared physical coverage. One currency and exact units; the total is rounded half-even to the contract's declared decimal places. Incremental amounts do not subtract history. Cumulative results separately preserve the assessed total and subtract the complete prior statements' incremental amounts to expose the current-period difference.

This mathematical method does not measure works, infer delay/disruption costs, determine entitlement or identify a payer. Real quantity/rate evidence, complete history, contract applicability and author permissions must be supplied and checked independently. No payment, debt, contract or work effect is performed.
