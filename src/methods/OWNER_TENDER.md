# Declared owner tender comparison

Use original eligibility/compliance and current authorized procurement budget before ranking. Rank only bids from one original round, package version and exact pricing basis. Every selected price/risk criterion has an explicit positive weight/scale and unit; exact rational weighted scores are minimized. Equal scores remain a group; serialization order within the group conveys no strict preference. A later award requires an explicit authorized selection and independent bidder acceptance.

Unknown qualification, price, compliance, funding or selected risk is a gap. A forecast is not spending authority or cash. Never infer exchange rates, adjustments, qualification, disclosure rights, technical conclusions or bidder commitments. Evaluation itself creates no contract, budget reservation, release of old commitments, or payment.
