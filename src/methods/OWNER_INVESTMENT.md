# Declared option intervals and cash calendars

These pure mathematical methods consume frozen authorized facts and explicit
host policy. They do not estimate forecasts, infer preferences, approve permits,
create investment authority, reserve cash or execute payments.

Option utility uses positive declared criterion weight divided by its positive
normalization scale. For a maximizing metric its lower bound contributes to the
conservative score and its upper bound to the optimistic score; for a minimizing
metric the negative upper/lower bounds contribute respectively. Sum these exact
fractions across the complete same-unit criterion set. Prefer the largest
conservative score; equal scores return every tied option for clarification.
Intervals express supplied uncertainty and do not imply probabilities. The
owner's three objective templates must use their own applicable criteria;
service/functional benefit cannot become sale revenue or spendable money.

The cash calendar keeps each exact funding source separate. At the current
business time start from its known actual free balance, already net of explicitly
disjoint current restrictions. Add only the host-verified future contractual
funding releases included by the processor at their availability time. Subtract
all existing outstanding portfolio outflows and the proposed option's outflows
at their declared times. Check every event endpoint: a later inflow never cures
an earlier deficit. Explicit same-time events settle together. A known unpaid
overdue obligation must be included at the current time; paid historical flows
must not be counted again. Do not repeat identities or copy the opening balance
in every funding line/window; accounts do not lend to each other without a
separately supported transfer mechanism.

Forecast income, an unaccepted finance offer, public service scores and an
unapproved budget are excluded from usable funding. Future committed availability
may support a conditional planning forecast after its required conditions have
been verified, but this calculation never records receipt or makes an actual
payment possible before funds arrive. Cash plans must retain input source,
period, currency, restrictions and existing debt lineage. Formal submission
must recheck the real shared ledger independently.

Support is mathematical and synthetic only: at most 64 options/funding sources,
32 criteria, one declared clock/currency, exact amounts, fixed resource capacity
handled by the separate capacity method. Professional qualification, cost and
schedule forecasts, weights, risk bounds, release reliability and registration
rules need real producers/calibration. Unsupported unit/currency/time conversions
must remain explicit.
