# Declared government rule check v1

Check disclosed quantities against explicitly sourced inclusive bounds in the exact declared unit, compare disclosed codes with an explicit required code, or combine independently verified professional findings. Applicability must have its own original basis. Unknown applicability remains unknown; an original inapplicability decision is not inferred from missing evidence.

Each required professional discipline/role remains separate. A nonconforming necessary check remains nonconforming even if another check conforms; unknown/missing coverage cannot yield conformity. A unit mismatch remains unknown without a conversion. This is exact mathematical checking of supplied evidence, not a real-world measurement, physical safety model, legal interpretation, weighted quality score or permit. Facts, rules, original professional executions and legal authority require independent host producers.
