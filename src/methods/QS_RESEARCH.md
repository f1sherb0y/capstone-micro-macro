# QS preliminary comparison methods v1

Consume saved scoped A04 totals. Never manufacture an estimate from a budget or fill unknown costs with zero. A comparison normalization uses the original saved total, a positive sourced FX coefficient and signed target-currency adjustments for every declared scope, region, time or performance/responsibility difference. Duplicate physical cost coverage remains an upstream error; normalization cannot make an incomplete scope into a complete estimate.

Ranking uses only comparable submitted plans with explicitly selected price/uncertainty criteria, positive weights and scales. Lower exact weighted sums are preferred. Equal scores remain tied and do not identify a unique preferred option. No selected ranking method or saved result means no ranking. All source limits, exclusions and professional gaps still apply.

Exact arithmetic uses fractions and finite decimal amounts. These are mathematical methods; they do not calibrate currencies, regional/time adjustments, scope valuations or uncertainty. A limited comparison is neither design feasibility nor investment approval. Repeated references do not create more research work, fees or expenditures.
