# Declared contractor bid arithmetic

Sum each original positive quantity multiplied by its nonnegative applicable unit rate in one declared currency. Coverage must be disjoint, units identical and all required included quantities accounted for. Offered price = internal cost subtotal × (1 + explicit commercial markup fraction) + explicit commercial allowance. A negative markup may express an authorized discount, but the factor and final price must remain positive. No missing value is treated as zero.

Productivity and resource plans use the independently declared work-method domain and exact capacity method. Neither arithmetic nor proposed tasks imply execution, construction release, formal reservation or actual expenses. Real rates, method applicability and professional uses require independent calibration and authorized sources.
