# Original payment valuation v1

Use original measured nonnegative work quantities, exact rate units and one contract currency. Round the assessed and eligible totals half-even to the declared precision. Unknown eligibility leaves entitlement/net amounts unknown while measured assessed value remains available. A failed condition produces a disputed amount and does not infer work acceptance.

Apply the original retention fraction and optional cap, individually authorized deductions and traced advance offsets. Negative current net is unsupported: excess credit/refund requires a separate decision. Cumulative totals subtract the complete prior certificates' current deltas. Incremental corrections subtract the replaced original certificate amount. Already paid certified cash remains in history; any excess becomes overpayment, never erased cash. Advance cash funds credits and is excluded from the certified-cash total so it cannot be deducted twice.

All results are calculations only. This method neither measures physical works nor certifies, creates an obligation, transfers cash, determines unknown rights or suspends valid work. Producers and the formal ledger must provide complete histories and enforce atomic correction and credit allocation.
