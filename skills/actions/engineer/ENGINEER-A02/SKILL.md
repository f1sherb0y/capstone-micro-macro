# ENGINEER-A02: professional service intake

## Goal and prerequisites

Assess the exact request and versioned terms using the authorized organization,
business clock and frozen read-only projections. This local deterministic
processor produces a decision; an application service and formal receipt are
still required for appointment, reservation or signing.

## Facts and member responsibilities

The service request identifies the client, payer, provider, their organizations,
commission/payment authorities and fee basis. Terms identify professional
disciplines, function, intended uses, responsibility, qualifications, methods,
fees, effort, work window and any independence requirement.
Professional members examine discipline, function, qualification and method
coverage. Resource members examine personnel suitability, shared commitments
and work-profile units. Commercial members examine sourced fee/risk limits and
versioned alternatives. Independence reviewers examine actual author
organizations and personnel through exact target versions and current relations.
Member opinions cannot replace these facts or create authority.

## Checks and decisions

1. Verify request/terms identity, scope, parties, current evidence and validity.
2. Ask registered blocking questions only of permitted client/payer recipients.
3. Apply the sourced new-work policy; distinguish decline from a resumable wait.
4. Check discipline and function coverage, current qualifications, methods,
   client commission authority and payer payment authority.
5. Read the exact independence requirement from both input and terms. An
   independent-review function requires a template. Supported local templates
   require separate personnel, or separate organization and personnel.
   Compare author organizations with the provider and its complete declared
   related-organization set. Compare authors with actual available personnel;
   changing agent_id cannot remove a relationship or create eligible capacity.
   A common payer alone neither proves nor disproves independence.
6. Exclude unsuitable or professionally uncovered personnel. Calculate constant
   capacity from effort / productivity / window duration, deduct commitments
   over every segment, and verify sourced fees and risk.
7. Accept only the exact supplied terms. For counteroffers, recheck every
   condition, previous version, calculation identity/time, method/discipline,
   calculated amount and a unique highest sourced priority.

## Missing facts and failures

Unknown facts produce needs_information; not_supported facts and unknown method
or template domains produce unsupported. Invalid identity/version/scope data
produces technical_error through the runner. Neither outcome contains acceptance.
Known ineligible terms decline with a reason, or use an eligible registered
counteroffer. Clarification contains the registered target and response
requirement. Defer retains a terms/policy dependency for later reassessment.

## Output and limits

Use only accept{terms_ref}, counteroffer{offer}, decline{reason}, or
clarify{questions} with the common scope and note. Rejection has no acceptance
fields. Capacity requests never imply a reservation, appointment, signing,
engineering result, payment, permit or successful independence review execution.

Local capacity support covers fixed region, one clock, same declared units and
half-open windows with constant productivity. Each counted resource must cover
the full requested discipline set; dividing effort among specialized teams needs
a separate work method. Relationship completeness and fee/professional method
facts are supplied evidence, not real-world accreditation. Synthetic tests prove
local rules and access wiring only; method calibration and macro binding remain
unverified.
