# QS-A02: service intake

Assess the exact service request and offered terms using authorized projections.
This action forms the QS organization's independent decision, not an appointment,
contract, cost approval, quantity certificate or resource reservation.

The scope reviewer verifies client/payer authority and permitted service uses;
the commercial reviewer reads the request's explicit fee basis and sourced fee
calculations; the capacity reviewer checks capability, qualifications, methods,
effort, calendar and existing commitments. The conflict reviewer consumes the
request-bound conflict record, its status, time, validity and evidence. All facts
and thresholds retain their sources; synthetic methods do not prove real QS
competence or valid fees. No write tools are declared.

1. Read the exact request, terms, capability and capacity in the frozen view.
   Preserve identities, scopes, units, clock and versions. Unreadable references
   or mismatched identities cannot become business acceptance.
2. Recheck client and payer authority, supported qualifications and methods,
   permitted scope and uses, sourced fee/risk policy and workload capacity.
   Unknown facts require information; unsupported methods retain their gap.
3. A known applicable conflict causes decline without retained acceptance.
   Unknown conflict requires information; a failed check is technical failure.
   Future, expired or unsupported-clock evidence cannot support independence.
4. Missing conflict_check_ref permits clarification or a sourced counteroffer,
   but acceptance waits for the conflict record. Do not fabricate a clear result.
5. Accept only the offered terms after all applicable checks. Decline on sourced
   adverse business facts. Clarify using registered targets, recipients and
   response requirements; retain the exact request and scope.
6. Counteroffers must use exposed, paired proposed terms and fee calculations.
   Revalidate their parties, scope, priority, method, amount, time, risk and
   capacity. Quotes do not assert independence or reserve resources.

Return the contract's accept, counteroffer, decline or clarify branch, or its
information, deferral, unsupported or technical state. Save auditable reads and
validation. Formal acceptance persistence, appointment, reservation, feedback
and receipt reconciliation require the actual macro binding.
