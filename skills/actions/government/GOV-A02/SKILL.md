# GOV-A02: submission intake

Assess a received submission against the exact, sourced intake procedure. This
action forms a local decision; formal receipt, case identity, fees and resource
assignments belong to the macro service.

Read only the current authorized submission, matter, jurisdiction, procedure,
agency authority, prior case, information requirements and their evidence.
Registration and operating regions do not confer jurisdiction over the project.
The intake officer checks identity and receipt, the jurisdiction reviewer checks
matter and project scope, and the checklist reviewer checks required materials,
signature and applicant eligibility. These deterministic checks share the same
contract for either organization strategy; no model reasoning proves compliance.

1. Preserve the submitted applicant, scope and reference versions. A prior case
   must explicitly link this submission. Unknown receipt or interface failure is
   technical failure requiring receipt reconciliation, not administrative refusal.
2. Check current matter and project jurisdiction. Outside coverage, use only an
   applicable sourced referral rule or a rule permitting decline. Unknown
   coverage or absent authority keeps an information gap.
3. Verify a proxy's authority at submission time. Missing or invalid authority
   requires information; do not fabricate the applicant's intent.
4. Follow the procedure's explicit receipt-only or completeness mode. Receipt
   acknowledgement neither accepts the application nor approves its substance.
5. Read every required checklist category and current material evidence. Missing
   documents or signature generate request_correction with the sourced question,
   recipient, response requirement and rule. Unknown eligibility remains unknown.
   Decline for known ineligibility or withdrawal requires its separate rule.
6. accept_for_processing requires the complete checklist, with no missing
   requirements or unresolved questions. Return the exact submission/procedure
   and scope; never report substantive approval, permission or completed review.

No write tools are declared. Unsupported schemas or clocks remain unsupported;
invalid identity/version relationships are technical errors. Do not retry formal
submissions, create cases, charge fees, reserve resources or issue permits here.
Preserve all formal binding, receipt, deduplication and feedback gaps in evidence.
