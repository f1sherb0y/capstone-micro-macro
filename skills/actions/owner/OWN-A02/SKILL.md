# OWN-A02 — Limited preliminary study and services

Use the host-selected Owner function, shared decision basics, current
authorization and frozen sourced facts. Read the exact private assessment case,
optional brief, registered issues, declared study profile and received service
offers. Without a brief, nonempty registered issues bound the research. No
formal project, final construction permit or financing receipt is needed for
authorized limited assessment. This does not authorize later investment,
unlimited consulting, construction or payment.

Delivery review verifies exact owner/case/brief/question versions, nonempty
work scope, current research permissions, fulfilled preliminary conditions,
future half-open study window and candidate identity. Work must answer the
candidate's selected registered questions: every work
item's question codes must belong to those questions, and together cover them.
An otherwise authorized work item cannot add an unrelated investigation.
Use only supplied bounded
internal, external or narrowing plans, at most 64 candidates. Branch identity
does not mix new internal allocations, a received service offer and a prior
study. Internal tasks have a local key; no fabricated Task ID is returned.

History review requires a complete current case study inventory. Retain existing
results, actual incurred fees and obligations with the original debtor, case
and any formal project lineage. Paid amounts require actual records; failure,
stopping, narrowing or later project formation never clears consulting debt.
Actual fees remain recorded even if they exceed a prior authorization cap;
an overrun does not turn those actual costs into zero.
Do not repeat an active study or charge for a previously studied question
without an explicit applicable reassessment trigger. Changed-information
triggers need new evidence references and content absent from the prior study's
information basis. A new identity or version carrying an old content hash is
not changed information. Scheduled review or failed-study
remedy must have a current verified requirement. Declared triggers are sourced
host assumptions, not inferred progress from the passage of another round.

Internal capability review checks the owner's available staff pools, assigned
function and scope authorization, qualification, supported questions and
complete current commitments. Aggregate all allocations to the same actual
pool. Invoke `micro.capacity.fixed_location:1` through the host MethodRegistry
to compare every half-open segment without copying physical capacity or
reserving resources. No undeclared unit/clock conversion is permitted.

External service review requires an actually received current offer with the
exact client, payer, case, work scope, questions, fee and delivery window.
Verify the professional author's organization, actor, function, applicable
authority, qualification and supported questions. A role label alone is not
qualification. `commission` is the owner's bounded choice; it never invents
the provider's independent acceptance or a formally effective contract.

Finance review reads current actual cash less explicitly disjoint committed
and restricted balances. Invoke `micro.owner.declared_study_budget:1` to check
the positive expenditure cap, spending limit, known future costs and existing
incurred costs. Select one feasible candidate using explicit ascending ordinal
priority. Equal best priorities request information, not an incidental order
choice. A narrowed cap includes already incurred costs, cannot exceed the
prior cap and strictly reduces the remaining scope; it creates no replacement
task, offer or payment and does not erase completed services.
The current authority must also cover the original work that narrowing would
remove; permission for the retained subset alone cannot cancel other work.

`internal_study` returns bounded scope/cap/window and nonempty proposed internal
allocations. `commission` returns scope/cap/window and the exact received offer.
`narrow` returns scope/cap/window and the exact active prior study. `stop` returns
only choice/note. Inapplicable known candidates may stop or defer according to
the sourced policy. Missing facts, unknown costs or authority request information;
unprovided methods or unit/clock conversions are unsupported. Preserve technical
failures. No-result and unchanged-history branches do not invent progress.

Recheck authority and facts before returning, and retain method audit inputs,
results and digests. This local decision creates no task, debt, expenditure,
resource reservation, permit, financing, project or macro receipt. Actual
input producers, formal task/commission submit/query, independent acceptance,
feedback, recovery and debt inheritance across project formation remain
unbound. CHECK-OWN-003/036/037/040 stay partial until those paths are verified.
