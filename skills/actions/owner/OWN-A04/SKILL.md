# OWN-A04 — Procure and commission explicitly scoped professional services

Act as the host-selected Owner function for the exact assessment, organization
or formal project scope. Read the sourced procurement profile, service scope,
responsibility matrix, deliverables, current capacity and commission authority.
They must refer to the same owner and service version. Professional advice is
not the owner's commercial acceptance, payment approval or regulatory permission.
Do not infer qualifications from a role label, registration location or company
description. Other explicitly permitted functions may provide services only when
their actual capabilities, qualifications, domains and authority are verified.

The delivery reviewer checks bounded work, exact deliverables, requested uses,
responsibilities, deadline and permitted authority. The procurement reviewer
checks eligible providers, received offers, authors and function/action grants,
qualification region and standing, required methods and their support domains.
The finance reviewer checks exact fees, payer identity and commercial authority
against the explicit procurement cap. A cap is not a cash balance or payment
instruction. Independent counterparties and the formal core still decide whether
an appointment becomes effective.

Use the explicit profile policy. `invite` selects only supplied eligible providers
with permission to receive this invitation; no offer is required. Investigating
applicable qualifications can be allowed even when appointment qualifications are
not yet known. `clarify` uses registered unresolved service issues and their exact
provider/offer recipients; no hidden or unrelated recipient is introduced.
`reject_all` and `retender` are valid with zero offers, preserve existing work and
obligations, and return only choice/note. No single remaining offer forces a deal.

For `accept` or the declared evaluation policy, examine all current received offers
on the exact employer/provider/payer, service scope, terms, responsibilities, uses,
deliverables, deadline and conditions. Skip withdrawn/expired offers and reselect
only current ones; unknown relevant fees, qualifications, methods, conditions or
preferences request information. Same-organization services use internal tasks,
not a self invoice or external appointment. Advice-only authority cannot expand
to procurement, construction, payment or regulatory permission. Through the host
MethodRegistry invoke `micro.owner.declared_service_selection:1`: exclude fees
above the same-currency cap and use explicit ascending ordinal priorities.
Ties request clarification; neither source order nor cheaper fees breaks a tie.

For `internal`, use only this organization's existing service functions,
capabilities and valid professional grants. Owner resource-arrangement authority
is checked separately from the actual staff's professional-service authority.
Retain existing-task references or bounded proposed task keys; a proposed key
cannot change scope within an output. Internal tasks collectively cover all
required work. Do not create an external invoice or treat an internal allocation
as a formal reservation.

Both acceptance and internal work must have complete current shared management
capacity, explicit management allocations covering the service, and actual owner
pools and commitments. Invoke `micro.capacity.fixed_location:1` on the exact
clock, units and half-open work window. Management and internal service demands
on one physical pool are aggregated, and prior commitments are counted once.
Known conflicts use the declared reject/retender/defer policy; unknown capacity
is missing information. Currency/unit/clock conversions and unavailable methods
are unsupported; technical failures stay technical.

Legal outputs contain only the source fields: invite/clarify recipient_ids and
issue_refs; accept exact offer_ref and terms_ref; internal allocations; or
reject_all/retender choice/note. Recheck all reading and authorization before
returning. Keep method inputs, reports and content digests in host audit. A local
completed decision creates no contract, appointment, payment, task, resource
commitment or receipt, and never accepts for another organization. Actual input
producers, appointment submission/query, independent acceptance, feedback and
recovery remain unbound. CHECK-OWN-010/015/058/061 remain partial until verified
through their real formal paths. Synthetic methods and facts do not prove real
professional qualification, productivity, cost calibration or model quality.
