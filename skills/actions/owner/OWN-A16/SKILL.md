# OWN-A16 — Bounded private proposal formation and revision

Use the host-selected Owner function, current authorization, shared decision
basics and frozen sourced projections. Read the exact private case, objective,
received demand/internal needs, explicit candidate space and current funding.
This action forms comparable private briefs. It does not create demand, acquire
land, finance work, select construction technology or establish a formal project.

Strategy review checks the case/objective versions and permitted purpose,
regions, interventions and alternatives. Market review uses the objective's
declared signal thresholds and maximum observation age. Learned/available times
must be no later than now; no future or other owner's private facts are used.
Own-use proposals require this owner's internal need for the exact case, region
and use. A public market signal alone never creates its expansion objective.
Every proposed region needs its own received qualifying signal within the
explicit objective and candidate-space bounds; the original case's screening
region is not an invented upper limit on the candidate space.
Public-service and investment candidates retain their distinct declared needs;
no common return formula, housing area or sales revenue is inferred.

Technical review checks the exact purpose/asset/intervention applicability of
the scale method. Use `micro.owner.declared_scale_bounds:1` through the host
MethodRegistry to compare supplied inclusive bounds in identical units. Unknown
endpoints remain unknown and carry distinct unresolved scale issues. A truly
non-applicable scale retains the authorized applicability rule on both endpoints;
it does not become zero. Road/bridge proposals may use declared connectivity or
length units and cannot silently receive building area. Technology alternatives
remain candidates; a brief may have no technology selected or named yet.

Rights review checks the exact case/purpose/regions and lawful research route.
Prospective rights or unknown holders remain explicit with an unresolved rights
issue. Existing third-party rights cannot be claimed as the owner's current
rights. No rights evidence requires an explicit unresolved path; research
permission never records a new exclusive right. Keep all unresolved issues in
the brief and do not turn private predictions into confirmed demand.

Finance and management review compare the current actual cash minus disjoint
committed/restricted cash and point-in-time available management effort with each
candidate's sourced review costs/effort. Use
the funding projection's explicit disjoint deduction policy; an unknown overlap
between commitments and restrictions requires information, not double deduction.
Use
`micro.owner.declared_proposal_priority:1` to choose explicit ascending ordinal
priorities subject to the declared brief-count maximum and joint review budgets.
Equal-priority groups that cannot fit together remain ambiguous; no incidental
list-order choice or invented utility is allowed. At most 64 candidates/briefs
are supported by this method. These are research comparisons, not spending,
resource reservations, project investment costs or financing forecasts.

`propose` returns nonempty bounded private briefs. `revise` returns substantively
changed briefs and one exact associated prior private brief per output; retain
the case identity, preserve historical versions and never create a duplicate
project by changing only a version number. Unchanged candidates produce no new
proposal; decimal spelling, reference-list order and measurement evidence alone
are not substantive changes. Repeated equivalent drafts under different local
identities are rejected. `no_proposal` carries only choice/note and does not require a prior
brief; known uninteresting/infeasible candidates do not force an experiment's
project count. Missing candidate-count limits or unprovided methods are
`unsupported`; missing needs, authorizations, costs and rights/scale issue
disclosures are `needs_information`. Method/transport/projection failures retain
their technical status and are never converted to no_proposal.

Recheck authority and frozen facts after calculations and preserve method audit
inputs/results/digests. Formal private brief registration, investment approval,
rights conflicts, submission/query, receipts, feedback and recovery still need
real host bindings. CHECK-OWN-021/022/029/048/067 remain partial until the relevant
formal lifecycle and real method calibration are independently verified.
