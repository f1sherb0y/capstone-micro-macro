# OWN-A01: observe opportunities

## Task and prerequisites

Screen authorized demand signals against the owner's sourced objective and
management load. The initial scope may be the organization; no project or
assessment case is required. A01 outputs a private assessment intent. Formal
case registration and later A16/A02 work require their own verified entry.

## Facts and responsibilities

Use exact OwnerObjective, DemandObservation, Observation, Opportunity,
OwnerCase and ManagementCapacity versions through the host reading boundary.
An objective names investment return, own use or public service, allowed
regions/uses, measured signal thresholds and a sourced new-signal policy.
These thresholds are scene assumptions, not universal business valuations.
The host verifies permissions and candidate exposure. The processor checks
identity, versions, sources, timing, objective fit and management load.
This deterministic Skill has no model or external tools.

## Decision procedure

1. Verify scope, objective/capacity ownership, effective windows and private
   case ownership. Repeated physical source/case IDs are invalid even if
   their versions differ. Preserve each observation's measurement and learned
   time; a later-published past measurement cannot enter an earlier decision.
2. Compare each signal independently with its declared threshold and unit.
   Never sum unlike statistics, replace missing values with zero or infer
   real demand from a template. Check explicit permission for preliminary
   research; this does not claim land, subsidy or construction permission.
3. Ignore known uninteresting signals. Request unknown/stale evidence; report
   missing unit or clock comparison support as unsupported. Multiple eligible
   regions/uses, opportunities or matching cases need a bounded selection.
4. Associate repeated signal/opportunity IDs with the existing private case.
   Follow unchanged source versions; revise changed or added sources on the
   same case. Reopening a closed case requires declared owner policy.
5. Before new or revised analysis, compare total management capacity with
   current commitments plus the screening requirement using exact quantities.
   Capacity exhaustion defers until a dependency change and revalidation;
   no capacity is reserved by this output.
6. Follow a currently researchable opportunity, or choose create_case/propose
   from the declared objective policy and nonempty linked Observation sources.
   No external opportunity is required for a sourced autonomous proposal.

## Output and boundaries

Return the frozen OwnerA01Payload branch and resolved evidence references, or
needs_information/deferred/unsupported/technical_error as appropriate. Follow
and revision targets must be supplied and exposed for that choice. Creation
sources use explicitly resolved Observation identities linked to the exact
DemandObservation versions, never a kind rename. Ignore does not delete shared
opportunities or discharge obligations. No project, exclusive right, funding,
research fee, commitment or successful macro receipt is created here.

Local tests use synthetic facts and declared thresholds. They do not establish
real demand forecasting, institutional valuation, model quality, stable case
registration, task persistence or a formal execution/feedback loop.
